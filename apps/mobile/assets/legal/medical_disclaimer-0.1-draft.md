# Medical disclaimer (draft)

DRAFT for review. Not final legal text.

Live Well supports healthy daily habits. It is not medical care.

Live Well does not diagnose illness, prescribe treatment, or tell you to start, stop or change any medication or supplement. Talk to your doctor or prescriber about those decisions.

If you have chest pain, signs of a stroke, severe trouble breathing, severe bleeding or fainting, call your local emergency number now.

If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact a crisis line or emergency services straight away. Live Well will show you the right numbers for where you live.

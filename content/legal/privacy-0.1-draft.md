# Privacy policy (draft)

DRAFT for review. Not final legal text.

We collect only what Live Well needs: your account details, the check-ins, habits and reflections you enter, and the choices you make in Privacy and consents.

You choose what Live Well may use, one switch at a time, and you can change your mind at any time. Each choice is saved with its date and the wording you saw.

Your health information is never sold, never used for advertising and never sent to the Live Well portal. Only anonymous tracking codes are shared with the portal when you open one of its pages from the app.

You can export your data or delete your account from My data. Face and voice models are deleted within 24 hours of your request.

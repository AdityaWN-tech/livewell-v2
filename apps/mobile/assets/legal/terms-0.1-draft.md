# Terms of use (draft)

DRAFT for review. Not final legal text.

Live Well is a wellness and habit companion based on Dr. Murthy Gokula's Live Well approach. It helps you build small daily habits, check in on how you feel and learn from approved content.

You must be 18 or older to use Live Well.

Live Well gives general wellness information. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it is not a substitute for your own doctor or clinician. Always talk to a clinician about medical decisions, including any change to medication.

Courses, programs and payments are provided by the Live Well portal under the portal's own terms. The app only links to them.

You can stop using Live Well and close your account at any time from My data.

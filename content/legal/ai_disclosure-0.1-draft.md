# AI disclosure (draft)

DRAFT for review. Not final legal text.

Your Live Well coach is an AI. Its replies are written by computer models and checked by safety rules before you see or hear them.

Health information from the coach comes only from approved Live Well content, such as Dr. Gokula's book and reviewed lessons, and shows its sources.

Voices in the app are AI voices and are labelled as such. A copied voice can only say messages made by Live Well, and can be deleted at any time.

The coach can make mistakes. It does not replace a clinician.
